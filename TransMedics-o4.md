## Executive Summary

• Over the past 12 months, TMDX shares have rallied 54.6% (3‑month gain) despite a mid‑cycle 50.5% drawdown from a $177.37 peak to $87.72 close on April 21, 2025 (vol. 885k) ([L151], [L163]).
• TransMedics’ Organ Care System (OCS™) ex‑vivo perfusion platform addresses the critical bottleneck in transplant logistics and organ discard rates. By 2021, >1,100 donor hearts were transplanted on OCS with survival benefits demonstrated in PROTECT II, PROCEED II and EXPAND‑Heart trials ([L76], [L77], [L78]).
• The core perfusion market was valued at USD 1.89 billion in 2023, forecast to USD 3.8 billion by 2035 at a 5.98% CAGR ([L54]). North America leads with ~42% share, rising to USD 1.6 billion by 2035 ([L55]).
• TransMedics guided 2024 revenue to $428–432 million (+77–79% y‑o‑y) and targets continued scale via its National OCS Program (NOP) and expanded reimbursement frameworks ([L34]).
• On May 11, 2023, TransMedics issued $460 million 1.50% convertible senior notes due 2028, generating net proceeds ~$393.3 million after issuance costs and capped calls ($52.1 M) used to fund logistics expansion and general corporate purposes ([L3], [L7], [L12], [L21]). The effective interest cost is ~2.1% reflecting amortization of discount ([L15]).
• We model three scenarios (Bull/Base/Bear), using DCF, EV/EBITDA and ROIC, arriving at a Base‑Case fair value of $115/share (24% upside). Risks include dilution, reimbursement delays, competitive dynamics and regulatory setbacks.

**Recommendation: BUY (12‑month target $115; 31x 2026 EV/EBITDA)**

## 3. Core Business Analysis

### 3.1 Product Portfolio

| Platform      | Indication                        | Key Differentiator                             |
|---------------|-----------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| OCS™ Heart    | Heart transplantation             | Warm perfusion; PROTECT II 2‑year survival 89% vs 79% ([L76])|
| OCS™ Lung     | Lung transplantation              | PROCEED II noninferiority; 30‑day survival 94% vs 97% ([L77])|
| OCS™ Liver    | Liver transplantation             | EXPAND‑Heart extended criteria: 6‑mo survival 88%, PGD 11% ([L78])|

### 3.2 Clinical Evidence & Adoption

- **PROTECT II (Heart):** 2‑year survival 89% (OCS) vs 79% (cold storage), similar PGD incidence ([L76]).
- **PROCEED II (Lung):** 30‑day survival 94% vs 97%; noninferiority at 2 years ([L77]).
- **EXPAND‑Heart:** Extended‑criteria donors: 30‑day survival 94%, 6‑mo survival 88%, severe PGD 11% ([L78]).
- **Registry Data:** Langmuur meta‑analysis: 1‑yr survival DBD+OCS 84%, DCD+OCS 89% vs static storage 87%; no difference in LOS or PGD at 4 years ([L79]).
- **Installed Base:** >1,100 hearts transplanted by 2021; >300 DCD hearts, reducing discard rates.

### 3.3 Reimbursement & Business Model

- **Model:** Sale of OCS console + disposable perfusion kits; recurring revenues from consumables.
- **Reimbursement:** Secured PMA approvals; Tamer Khayal’s team established CPT codes and payor coverage; NOP incentivizes volume-based pricing.
- **Logistics Network:** Investment of ~$400 M from convertible proceeds to expand supply chain, training, and service centers across key transplant hubs.

**Key Drivers:** Organ shortage, favorable clinical data, shift to normothermic preservation, expanding indications (kidney, pancreas) and AI/ML perfusion monitoring integration.

## 4. Competitive Landscape & Industry Overview

### 4.1 Market Sizing & Growth Drivers

- **Global Perfusion Systems Market (MRFR):** USD 1.89 B in 2023 → USD 3.8 B by 2035; 5.98% CAGR (2025–35) ([L54]).
- **Alternate Forecast (2024–32):** USD 1.35 B in 2023 → USD 2.02 B by 2032; 4.5% CAGR ([L56]).

### 4.3 SWOT Summary

**Strengths:** Clinical survival advantage (OCS), PMA approvals across three organs, recurring consumables, strong IP.
**Weaknesses:** High console cost; longer training; negative free cash flow pre‑scale; mixed employee sentiment.
**Opportunities:** New organ indications (kidney, pancreas), AI/ML integration, emerging markets, partnerships with logistics/aviation.
**Threats:** HMP incumbents (XVIVO), hypothermic alternatives (SherpaPak), reimbursement delays, potential clinical complications or product recalls.
