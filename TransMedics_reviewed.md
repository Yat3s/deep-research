## 3  Core Business Analysis <a id="3-core-business-analysis"></a>

### 3.1  Product & Service Portfolio

- **OCS Heart / Lung / Liver** — extends preservation (heart ≤ 12 h, lung 24–36 h, liver ≥ 24 h).
- **National OCS Program (NOP)** — 17 regional hubs, 19 aircraft, turnkey logistics; lowers “mission loss” to **<5 %** (company data, not peer‑reviewed).
- **Revenue mix 2024:** Disposables **62 %** / Services **38 %**.

### 3.2  Clinical Outcomes (key trials)

| Trial                              | Endpoint                      | OCS                   | Cold Storage           |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | --------------------- | ---------------------- |
| **PROTECT (Liver)**                | Early allograft dysfunction   | 17.3 %                | 30.5 % (p=0.009) [S12] |
|                                    | Ischemic cholangiopathy       | 1.4 %                 | 8.5 % (p=0.005)        |
| **INSPIRE / EXPAND (Lung, Heart)** | 1‑year survival               | ~94 %                 | ~92 % (NS) [S13]       |
|                                    | Utilization of ECD/DCD donors | ↑ 30–50 % vs. control |

### 3.3  Growth Roadmap

- Next‑gen OCS (24 h heart/lung) — 2025 pilot.
- Int’l rollout (EU/APAC) — initial liver approvals filed.
- AI‑enabled organ‑quality analytics — partnership/Bolt‑on M&A under evaluation.

> **Moat:** First‑to‑market, FDA‑approved multi‑organ platform + proprietary logistics network yield a **high‑switching‑cost ecosystem**.

## 7  Conclusion <a id="7-conclusion"></a>

TransMedics remains the **category‑defining leader** in portable, multi‑organ normothermic perfusion, now profitable with a runway to >$1 B revenue by 2028. Nevertheless, **valuation, legal uncertainty, service‑mix margin pressure and cultural turnover** warrant a balanced stance. Investors should track upcoming catalysts:

- **Q1 2025 earnings release — 2025‑04‑29**
- **CFO succession announcement (H1 2025)**
- **Court rulings / DOJ updates (ongoing 2025)**

Risk‑tolerant holders may view pullbacks toward EV/S < 6× FY25E as attractive; risk‑averse investors could await legal clarity or margin stabilization.
